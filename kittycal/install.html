On an iPhone

Takes about twenty seconds.

  1. 1 Open the link in Safari. Scanning the QR code with the camera does this for you.
  2. 2 Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — a square with an arrow coming out of it.
  3. 3 Scroll down the grey list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4 Tap Add. That's it — Kittycal is now an icon on your Home Screen.

It has to be Safari. If you tapped the link inside Instagram, TikTok or Messages, you're in their mini-browser and there's no Add to Home Screen. Tap the Open in Safari button first, then start again.

On an Android

Same idea, one step shorter.

  1. 1 Open the link in Chrome. Scanning the QR code with the camera does this for you.
  2. 2 Tap the menu — three dots in the top-right corner.
  3. 3 Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen if that's what yours says.
  4. 4 Confirm with Install, and the icon appears in your app drawer.

Chrome sometimes offers this to you on its own as a little Install bar at the bottom of the page. Tapping that does exactly the same thing.

Once it's on your Home Screen

  • ✓ Open it from the icon, not the browser. That's what keeps your data safely stored instead of being cleared automatically.
  • ✓ It works with no signal at all — on a plane, in a basement, anywhere.
  • ✓ Tell it roughly when your last period started and how long your cycle usually is. Two questions, then you're done.
  • ✓ Tap the ? at the top of the app any time — it explains every screen, including how the predictions are worked out.
  • ✓ Everything lives on your phone, so back it up now and then with Settings → Export everything.
Kittycal is not contraception and doesn't diagnose anything. Predictions are estimates from your own history.
